27 verbs conjugated in full. 45 verbs in the quick-reference index. 10 tenses, six persons,

Grouped into 5 patterns instead of an alphabetical list:
• Truly irregular - ser, estar, ir, haber, dar, ver, saber
• Irregular yo - tener, hacer, poner, salir, venir, decir, traer, oír, conocer
• Stem change e to ie - pensar, querer, empezar, sentir
• Stem change o to ue - poder, dormir, volver, encontrar
• Stem change e to i - pedir, seguir, servir

Learn one pattern and you get four verbs, not one.
